Enough Money.

No matter how much money you make, it is enough.

You have enough money. The hard part is convincing yourself that the lifestyle that your money provides is worth living. This app won't help you make more money, or change your thoughts about your lifestyle. It will help you build habits about money saving and spending so you can live within your means. The goal of this app is to help you see what money you have each month, decide how to spend it, and then try to keep with that plan. At the end of each month, you'll see that you do have enough money.

Why it's different

1

Every month starts with a plan

When a new month begins, Enough Money asks you to make a plan before you spend: how much you mean to use from each envelope. Start fresh, copy last month's plan, or use what you actually spent last month. All month long, Spent turns orange when it goes past Planned, so you can see where you're drifting from the plan while there's still time to adjust.

2

Envelopes can't go negative

Every envelope holds real money. If a purchase is more than an envelope holds, you add money to the envelope first: from Available to Assign, from that envelope's Saved, or from another envelope. An envelope is never in the red, not even for a moment.

3

No autopilot

Enough Money never connects to your bank. You type in what you spend, or bring in your bank's file yourself. For every transaction, you pick an envelope or mark it as Income.

4

Leftover money becomes Saved

Money you don't spend this month isn't quietly rolled into next month. It moves into the envelope's Saved, marked with sparkles, and keeps growing. To spend it you have to move it out on purpose, so dipping into savings is always a choice.

5

Debt stays in view

What you owe to your credit cards sits at the top of every page, in red, until it's gone.

How it works

Set up once

  1. Add your accounts: checking and credit cards, with today's balances.

  2. Create envelopes: groceries, rent, gas, fun, grouped however you like.

Every month

  1. Make a plan. Decide how much money you plan to use from each envelope this month, and enter it as that envelope's Planned amount. In later months, you can start a new plan, copy last month's plan, or use last month's actual spending as your new plan.

  2. Assign your money to envelopes. Tap the green banner and assign money to envelopes until the banner disappears.

  3. Record what you spend. Tap +, enter the amount and pick the envelope. Or import your bank's file and sort it.

  4. No money, no spending. If an envelope doesn't have enough for a transaction, the transaction can't be saved until you add enough to the envelope: move money from another envelope, release some of that envelope's Saved, or assign more from Available to Assign. You can't spend money you don't have.

  5. Review imported transactions. If you import transactions from a CSV file, assign each one to an envelope and mark paychecks as Income.

  6. Reconcile with statements. Periodically reconcile the app with the bank and card statements to ensure accuracy.

  7. Watch your savings grow. Whatever you don't spend is next month's Saved.

FAQ

Why doesn't it connect to my bank?
On purpose. Typing or reviewing each transaction keeps you aware of your money. It also means your bank login never leaves your bank.
Is my data private?
Only people in your household can see your budget. There are no ads, and nothing is sold or shared. You can export everything to a backup file at any time.
Can my spouse or partner use it too?
Yes. Everyone in a household signs in to the same budget.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. It runs in the browser and can be added to your home screen like an app. It works on a computer too.
Can I import from my bank?
Yes. Download a CSV file from your bank's website and import it. The app skips transactions it already has.
What about credit cards?
Each card gets its own payment envelope that fills as you spend, so the money to pay the bill is already set aside.
