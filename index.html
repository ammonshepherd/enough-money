Envelope budgeting

Give every dollar a job. Keep what you don't spend.

Enough Money is an envelope budget for your household. Put money in envelopes, spend from them, and watch what's left pile up as savings, safely out of reach until you choose to use it.

Open the app Read the guide

Works on your phone, tablet or computer, and installs on your home screen. Accounts are by invitation.

How an envelope works

Three numbers, and nothing hidden

Every envelope, every month, keeps the same simple story. The arithmetic never changes and never goes below zero.

Assigned

The money you give the envelope this month, from Available to Assign.

Remaining

Assigned, minus what you spend. This is what you can spend right now.

Saved

Whatever is left at the end of a month moves here and keeps growing, month after month.

What's different

Built around the habits that make budgets work

Most budgeting apps track where money went. Enough Money is built to help you decide where it goes, and to stop you quietly undoing those decisions.

Saved is a reserve

Unspent money is saved, not spent by accident

In many envelope apps, leftover money simply rolls into next month's balance, where it is easy to spend without noticing. Here it moves into the envelope's Saved reserve, marked with a purple pill and a little sparkle.

Saved money still belongs to the envelope, but you can only spend it after you choose Move some into Remaining. Dipping into savings is always a decision, never a surprise.

No negative envelopes

Overspend? Cover it now, not later

An envelope can never go below zero. If a purchase is bigger than what's left, the app asks you to cover the difference before it saves, always in the same order:

  1. release some of this envelope's Saved,
  2. move money from another envelope,
  3. assign more from Available to Assign.

You always know where the money came from, and there's never a red number waiting to be cleaned up later.

Credit cards, handled

Every card gets its own payment envelope

Each credit card has a payment envelope that fills up on its own. Buy groceries on the card and the money moves from Groceries into the card's payment envelope, so the cash to pay the bill is already set aside.

The envelope shows Available to pay and any Remaining debt the envelope can't cover yet. A Credit card debt banner on every page keeps the total in view. Refunds and statement credits just lower what you owe.

Review before it counts

Nothing counts until you've looked at it

Imported transactions arrive Uncategorized and wait in a To review list. A paycheck doesn't add to your budget until you mark it as Income, and a purchase doesn't leave an envelope until you pick one.

Your budget only moves when you decide. Income rules can mark regular paychecks for you as they're imported.

Your bank's file, no connection

Import your bank's CSV, never twice

Download a CSV file from your bank or card website and choose it. Columns are detected for you and remembered for each account. Your bank login never touches the app.

Rows already imported are skipped. A bank row that matches a transaction you typed in yourself is matched to it, keeping your envelope and notes, instead of being added a second time.

Made for thumbs

A number pad that does the sums

On a phone, tapping Assigned opens a number pad along the bottom of the screen. Type a new total, or tap + or − to add to or take from what's already there. "500.00 + 25.00 = $525.00", no mental arithmetic.

Amounts fill in cents first, like a cash register: type 1, 2, 3 and you get $1.23.

And the rest

The details are thought through

Transfers aren't spending

Moving money between your accounts, or paying a card, is one linked entry. It's never counted as income or spending.

Split a purchase

One store receipt, several envelopes: groceries and a gift in a single entry.

Planned is just a plan

Write down what you expect to spend. Spent turns orange when it passes the plan, without blocking anything.

Start next month in one tap

A new month offers to copy last month's plan or last month's spending.

Tracking accounts

Follow a loan or investment account's balance without it touching your budget.

Mark it reconciled

Tick off rows that match your statement; a green bank icon shows what's checked.

Shared with your household

Everyone in the household sees the same budget. No one outside it can see a thing.

Your data, in one file

Export everything to a single backup file at any time from Settings.

Phone first, desk friendly

Install it like an app on your phone. On a computer it spreads out, with a sidebar and a side panel.
